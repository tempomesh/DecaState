## 1. Mathematical Foundations of Self-Attention

The core mechanism of a Transformer is **Self-Attention**, which calculates the contextual relevance of words in a sequence using Query ($\mathbf{Q}$), Key ($\mathbf{K}$), and Value ($\mathbf{V}$) representations.

### Master Formula

$$\text{Attention}(\mathbf{Q}, \mathbf{K}, \mathbf{V}) = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)\mathbf{V}$$

---

### Step 1: Compute Raw Attention Scores ($\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T$)

Given:
* Query vector ($\mathbf{Q}$, shape $1 \times 2$): $\mathbf{Q} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ (for token `"tea"`)
* Key vectors matrix ($\mathbf{K}$, shape $2 \times 3$): 
  $$\mathbf{K} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \quad \text{where } \mathbf{K}_{\text{I}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \mathbf{K}_{\text{like}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \mathbf{K}_{\text{tea}} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Multiplying $\mathbf{Q}$ by $\mathbf{K}$ computes dot products across all tokens simultaneously:

$$\mathbf{Q} \mathbf{K}^T = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

* **"I":** $(1 \cdot 1) + (1 \cdot 0) = 1$
* **"like":** $(1 \cdot 0) + (1 \cdot 1) = 1$
* **"tea":** $(1 \cdot 1) + (1 \cdot 1) = 2$

$$\text{Raw Scores Matrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \quad (\text{shape: } 1 \times 3)$$

---

### Step 2: Scale the Scores ($\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}$)

Scale the raw attention scores by $\sqrt{d_k} = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$ to prevent large values and ensure stable gradient backpropagation:

$$\text{Scaled Scores} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{1.414} & \frac{1}{1.414} & \frac{2}{1.414} \end{bmatrix} \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.707 & 0.707 & 1.414 \end{bmatrix}$$

---

### Step 3: Softmax Probability Normalization

The **Softmax** function exponentiates each value ($e^{x_i}$) to convert negative/neutral values into positive terms, then divides each by the total sum ($\sum e^{x_j}$) to produce a valid probability distribution summing to $1.0$ ($100\%$).

#### 1. Calculate Exponentials ($e^{x_i}$)
* $e^{0.707} \approx 2.028$
* $e^{0.707} \approx 2.028$
* $e^{1.414} \approx 4.112$

#### 2. Sum Exponentials ($\sum e^{x_j}$)
$$\sum = 2.028 + 2.028 + 4.112 = 8.168$$

#### 3. Compute Probabilities ($\frac{e^{x_i}}{\sum e^{x_j}}$)
* **First element ("I"):** $\frac{2.028}{8.168} \approx 0.2483 \rightarrow \mathbf{24.8\%}$
* **Second element ("like"):** $\frac{2.028}{8.168} \approx 0.2483 \rightarrow \mathbf{24.8\%}$
* **Third element ("tea"):** $\frac{4.112}{8.168} \approx 0.5034 \rightarrow \mathbf{50.4\%}$

#### Softmax Matrix Vector
$$\text{Softmax}(\begin{bmatrix} 0.707 & 0.707 & 1.414 \end{bmatrix}) \approx \begin{bmatrix} 0.248 & 0.248 & 0.504 \end{bmatrix}$$

---

### Step 4: Mix Value Vectors ($\text{Softmax} \times \mathbf{V}$)

Multiply the $1 \times 3$ Softmax probability vector by the $3 \times 2$ Value matrix ($\mathbf{V}$) to compute the weighted contextual representation:

Given Value matrix $\mathbf{V}$:
$$\mathbf{V} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \quad \left(\mathbf{V}_{\text{I}} = [1, 0], \mathbf{V}_{\text{like}} = [0, 1], \mathbf{V}_{\text{tea}} = [1, 1]\right)$$

$$\text{Attention Output} = \begin{bmatrix} 0.248 & 0.248 & 0.504 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

* **Weighted $\mathbf{V}_{\text{I}}$:** $0.248 \times [1, 0] = [0.248, 0.000]$
* **Weighted $\mathbf{V}_{\text{like}}$:** $0.248 \times [0, 1] = [0.000, 0.248]$
* **Weighted $\mathbf{V}_{\text{tea}}$:** $0.504 \times [1, 1] = [0.504, 0.504]$

$$\text{Attention Output} = \begin{bmatrix} (0.248 + 0 + 0.504) & (0 + 0.248 + 0.504) \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{\begin{bmatrix} 0.752 & 0.752 \end{bmatrix}} \approx \mathbf{[0.75, 0.75]}$$
