DecaState owns: routing, hashes, evidence, metrics, policy  ·  Provider owns: model execution + server-side KV/prompt cache  ·  Model owns: the reasoning and the answer
⚠ Stated plainly: DecaState's gateway runs on your localhost — one line to switch (ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL / OpenAI base_url), streaming relayed byte-for-byte, both providers. Your prompts never leave your machine except straight to the provider you chose with your key — nothing to attest, because nothing is hosted by us. A ChatGPT/Codex or Claude subscription session is provider-managed: no external tool can intercept it or prove dollar savings there.
